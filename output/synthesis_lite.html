完整研判分析

美國經濟正處於舒適區——增長溫和加速，核心通脹逐步降溫。此背景讓風險資產蓬勃發展：股市創新高，信貸利差是十年來最窄，VIX恐慌指數舒適地低於17。然而表面之下，債券市場正在定價不同的故事：未來一年將有一至兩次升息，驅動力是仍然偏高的整體通脹與堅挺的勞動市場。結果是殖利率曲線的熊市平坦化，即短期利率上升速度快於長期利率，這是市場預期聯儲局在經濟仍健康時收緊的典型訊號。

今日的關鍵矛盾是強勢美元。隨著美國利率相對於世界其他地區上升，美元已飆升至數月高位，將日圓與歐元大幅壓低。歷史經驗顯示，如此的美元強勢會收緊全球金融環境，並壓抑新興市場與商品價格。目前美股對其不以為意，但若美元繼續攀高，可能成為企業盈利的逆風，最終觸發避險情緒。投資者應關注美元指數的見頂，因為那將是緊縮週期已完全定價的訊號。

觸發風險 · 若美元指數突破105且2-10年期公債利差降至20個基點以下，則應為金融環境急劇收緊引發的大盤修正做好準備。

長期結構定位
現在在大週期的哪裡

我們在大週期的哪個位置？聯儲局資產負債表穩定，過去三個月淨流動性大致持平。然而自2025年中以來的趨勢為緩慢擴張，M2年增率5.6%，對資產價格構成順風。利率期貨市場現隱含2026年12月前升息25個基點並延續至2027年初，表示即便通脹正在放緩，聯儲局仍預期將對抗通脹。殖利率曲線處於熊市平坦化階段，短期利率上升速度快於長期利率，此結構常預示政策利率見頂與最終衰退風險。對於多年期投資者，此背景暗示週期正走向成熟：儘管眼前環境有利於風險資產，緊縮路徑與平坦曲線提高了未來兩到三年內衰退的機率，審慎的做法是逐步轉向較高品質資產。

歷史借鏡解讀

Narrative

The US economy is in a sweet spot — growth is accelerating modestly, while core inflation is gradually cooling. This backdrop has allowed risk assets to thrive: stocks are at records, credit spreads are the tightest in a decade, and the market volatility sits comfortably below 17. Yet beneath the surface, the bond market is pricing a different story: one or two rate hikes over the next year, driven by still-high headline inflation and a labour market that refuses to crack. The result is a bear flattening of the yield curve, where short-term rates rise faster than long-term rates, a classic signal that markets expect the Fed to tighten into an otherwise healthy economy.

The key tension today is the strong dollar. As US rates rise relative to the rest of the world, the dollar has surged to its highest in months, pushing the yen and euro sharply lower. Historically, such dollar strength tightens global financial conditions and pressures emerging markets and commodity prices. For now, US equities are shrugging it off, but if the dollar continues to climb, it could become a headwind for corporate earnings and eventually trigger a risk-off episode. Investors should watch for a peak in the dollar index, as that would be a signal that the tightening cycle is fully priced.

Trigger risk · If the dollar index breaks above 105 and the 2-10 year yield spread flattens below 20 basis points, then prepare for a broader market correction as financial conditions tighten sharply.

Structural Backdrop
Where we stand in the big cycle

Where are we in the big cycle? The Federal Reserve's balance sheet is stable, with net liquidity roughly flat over the past three months. However, the broader trend since mid-2025 has been one of slow expansion, with M2 growing at 5.6% year-over-year — a tailwind for asset prices. The rate futures market now implies a 25-basis-point hike by December 2026, extending into early 2027, meaning the Fed is still expected to lean against inflation even as it decelerates. The yield curve is in a bear flattening phase, where short-term rates are rising faster than long-term rates, a configuration that often precedes a policy peak and eventual recession risk. For multi-year investors, this backdrop suggests that the cycle is maturing: while the immediate environment is favourable for risk assets, the path of tightening and a flat curve raise the odds of a downturn within the next two to three years, making it prudent to gradually rotate toward higher-quality assets.

The current setup most closely echoes May 2007. Credit spreads were extremely tight, volatility was low, and the yield curve had flattened dramatically. The most important difference is that today’s liquidity backdrop is less extreme — the Fed’s balance sheet is not shrinking rapidly, and the banking system is healthier. If this episode follows a similar path, we would expect a period of calm followed by a sharp repricing of risk sometime in the next 6-12 months — but a recession is not imminent as long as the labour market holds up.
