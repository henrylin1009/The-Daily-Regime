完整研判分析

總經數據符合金髮女孩標籤：成長加速，通膨從去年峰值回落。但核心價格仍居高不下，聯準會暫停升息，市場卻定價兩次額外升息。這種分歧顯示政策可能落後於通膨再次加速，最終被迫更激進轉向。

全球方面，日圓極端疲弱催生了危險擁擠的利差交易。一旦突然逆轉，可能波及股市和債市。投資者自滿——可從歷史低位的信用利差看出——是一大警訊，與2007年前夕相似。降低過度風險暴露、持有流動性是合理的。

觸發風險 · 若通膨數據意外上升或聯準會暗示更陡峭的升息路徑，則可能出現債券拋售、美元飆升及風險資產大幅回調。

長期結構定位
現在在大週期的哪裡

我們仍處於景氣擴張後期，聯準會轉為暫停但市場已定價升息。央行資產負債表穩定在6.74兆美元，淨流動性持平——2022–23年的快速收縮已結束。然而，聯邦基金期貨曲線隱含明年三月利率接近4.1%，顯示通膨（PCE 4.1%）不允許轉向鴿派。殖利率曲線呈牛市陡峭化，2至10年期利差為+0.45個百分點，反映成長樂觀與遲續的利率擔憂。對多年期投資人而言，這意味超低利率時代已遠去；實質收益率將持續對長天期資產構成逆風，而實物資產和短天期債券提供相對避險。

The most important market move today
Rate hike bets surge, yen plunges further

The New Zealand dollar/yen cross surged 2.7% in a day while the U.S. dollar pushed above 163 yen, reflecting a resurgent carry trade as funding currencies sell off. At the same time, Fed funds futures now price in one rate hike by December and another by March, stoking bond market volatility.

Narrative

The macro picture fits the Goldilocks label: growth is accelerating, and headline inflation is easing from last year's peak. Yet core prices remain stubborn, and the Fed is on hold while the market prices in two more hikes. This split reveals a risk that policy will lag behind a reacceleration in inflation, forcing a sharper turn later.

Globally, the yen's extreme weakness has spawned a dangerously crowded carry trade. A sudden unwind could roil everything from stocks to bonds. Investor complacency—visible in historically tight credit spreads—is a red flag, echoing pre‑2007 conditions. Reducing excessive risk exposure and holding liquidity makes sense.

Trigger risk · If inflation data surprises to the upside or the Fed signals a steeper rate path, expect a bond sell-off, dollar surge, and sharp correction in risk assets.

Structural Backdrop
Where we stand in the big cycle

We remain in a late-cycle expansion where the Fed has shifted to hold but the market is pricing hikes. The central bank’s balance sheet is stable at $6.74 trillion, with net liquidity flat—the rapid drainage of 2022–23 has ended. However, the fed funds futures curve implies a rate near 4.1% by March, signaling that inflation (PCE 4.1%) isn’t allowing a dovish turn. The yield curve is bull-steepening with a 2–10 year spread of +0.45 percentage points, reflecting growth optimism and lingering rate fears. For multi-year investors, this means the era of ultra-low rates is solidly behind us; real yields will remain a headwind for long-duration assets, while hard assets and short-term paper offer relative refuge.

Historical Context

The current setup most closely echoes early 2007: a Goldilocks regime with low volatility, tight credit spreads, and a steepening yield curve, yet under the surface, positioning was stretched and rate expectations began to shift. Back then, the Fed was on hold while inflation worries lingered, similar to today’s mix of slowing headline CPI but sticky core prices. The key difference is that banking system leverage is lower now, reducing systemic fragility, but the crowded yen carry trade and hedging anomalies suggest pockets of risk. If this episode follows the 2007 pattern, watch for a rapid repricing of rate expectations that could trigger a credit or equity shakeout—but central banks may be quicker to respond this time.
