美國殖利率飆升，曲線陡峭化

美國10年期殖利率本週上升2.6%至4.67%，2至10年期利差擴大到0.63個百分點，出現熊市陡峭化。這顯示市場正在定價更高的通膨或更快的聯準會緊縮，挑戰金髮女孩情境。

完整研判分析

官方宏觀標籤為金髮女孩，反映穩健成長與核心通膨放緩。但近距離觀察：整體通膨仍高達3.7%以上，聯準會偏好的通膨指標超過4%，且期貨市場現已定價至2027年初前升息兩次。殖利率曲線轉為熊市陡峭化，清楚顯示債市投資人擔憂通膨升溫或更快的緊縮循環。同時，股市與信用市場未見恐懼，仍在極端水準交易。

這種分歧正是關鍵張力所在。平靜的表面——低VIX、緊縮的信用利差——正受到避險需求上升、石油波動率因供給憂慮飆升，以及日圓利差交易極端布局的侵蝕。若聯準會被迫升息，或發生地緣政治衝擊，這些擁擠的交易可能劇烈平倉，懲罰那些定價完美的風險資產。

觸發風險 · 若整體通膨未能趨緩，且聯準會暗示更快緊縮路徑，則債券殖利率將進一步飆升，股市可能修正，美元可能走強，傷害新興市場。

長期結構定位
現在在大週期的哪裡

聯準會的淨流動性仍在擴張，過去13週增加2,160億美元，受資產負債表微幅上升與近乎零的逆回購餘額推動，這提供了寬鬆的環境，即使利率期貨指向更高。然而，殖利率曲線正處於熊市陡峭化——長天期利率上升快於短天期——這通常預示市場開始定價更高的通膨或成長預期，而非衰退。利率期貨曲線隱含至2027年初再升息兩次，政策利率將從目前的3.63%升至約4.1%。這種結構性背景顯示，雖然流動性充裕，但方向正轉向未來一年更趨緊縮的金融條件，這將在多年期內日益挑戰資產估值。

Narrative

The official macro label is Goldilocks, reflecting solid growth and decelerating core inflation. But look closer: headline inflation is still running hot at over 3.7%, the Fed's preferred measure is above 4%, and the futures market now prices two rate hikes by early 2027. The yield curve has shifted into a bear steepener, a clear signal that bond investors fear either higher inflation or a faster tightening cycle. Meanwhile, stocks and credit show no fear, trading at extremes.

This divergence is the key tension. The calm surface — low market volatility, tight credit spreads — is undermined by rising hedging demand, a spike in oil volatility due to supply fears, and extreme positioning in the yen carry trade. If the Fed is forced to hike, or if a geopolitical shock hits, the crowded trades could unwind violently, punishing risk assets that are priced for perfection.

Trigger risk · If headline inflation fails to moderate and the Fed signals a faster tightening path, then bond yields will spike further, stocks will likely correct, and the dollar may strengthen, hurting emerging markets.

Structural Backdrop
Where we stand in the big cycle

The Fed's net liquidity is still expanding, with a $216 billion increase over the past 13 weeks, driven by a slight uptick in the balance sheet and near-zero reverse repo balances. This provides a loose backdrop even as rate futures point higher. However, the yield curve is bear steepening — long-term rates are rising faster than short-term rates — which typically signals that markets are beginning to price higher inflation or growth expectations rather than a recession. The rate futures curve implies two additional hikes by early 2027, bringing the policy rate near 4.1% from today’s 3.63%. This structural backdrop suggests that while liquidity is ample, the direction is shifting toward tighter financial conditions over the next year, which will increasingly challenge asset valuations in a multi-year horizon.

Historical Context

The current setup most closely echoes mid-2007, when a Goldilocks backdrop nurtured complacency: low volatility, tight credit spreads, and elevated equities, while underlying stresses built in housing and credit markets. The key similarity today is the extreme tightness of credit spreads and record stock levels against a backdrop of rising bond yields and hidden geopolitical risks. The critical difference is that today’s banking system is far more robust, and the economy is not as levered to a single sector. If this episode follows a similar path, we should watch for a sudden liquidity event or policy error that cracks the calm — but a strong labor market and Fed put could limit the downside.
