完整研判分析

當前的美國宏觀環境為金髮女孩：增長加速，核心通膨放緩，讓聯準會暫時按兵不動。這使股市保持活躍，信貸利差狹窄，標普500創歷史新高。然而，勞動市場顯示降溫跡象，6月新增就業僅5.7萬人，最終可能限制增長。

緊張之處在於整體通膨持續高於4%（CPI 4.27%、PCE 4.07%），這迫使聯邦基金期貨定價年底前加息。與此同時，債市波動率暴跌，表明市場對利率路徑普遍感到安心，但避險成本（SKEW上升）卻在增加，顯示機構謹慎。美元走強也給新興市場和融資貨幣帶來壓力，造成全球分化。

觸發風險 · 若整體通膨未能放緩，且聯準會真的加息，則股市估值，尤其昂貴的科技股，可能遭遇劇烈重估，且日圓可能因利差交易平倉而迅速走強。

長期結構定位
現在在大週期的哪裡

美國經濟正處於持續數月的金髮女孩中期階段，由加速成長和核心通膨放緩支撐。聯準會資產負債表穩定（總資產6.736兆美元，13週增0.5%），淨流動性持平於5.956兆美元，逆回購工具幾近耗盡至60億美元，M2年增率穩健達5.6%。利率期貨曲線暗示聯準會年底前將升息26個基點，隱含利率達3.96%，為鷹派重定價。殖利率曲線呈熊市陡峭化，2-10年期利差62個基點，反映市場預期較高的成長和通膨溢酬。此結構背景預示著從流動性高峰階段向緊縮週期過渡——投資人應為實質利率逐步上升和更具選擇性的環境做好準備，未來數年債券可能表現遜色，股市領導地位或將輪動，遠離對存續期間敏感的類股。

歷史借鏡解讀

當前的宏觀環境最接近2007年中，當時同樣是金髮女孩格局，低波動且信貸利差狹窄。彼時聯準會按兵不動，股市處於高點，自滿情緒普遍，但殖利率曲線趨平，避險需求悄然上升。今天的關鍵相似點是強勁的風險偏好、低波動和狹窄信貸，伴隨機構避險活動（SKEW上升）暗示尾部風險被低估。最重要的不同是曲線在陡峭化而非平坦，且銀行體系資本更充足。若本輪循2007年路徑，應警惕信貸風險突然重定價重創股市，但當前的宏觀動能暗示軟著陸可能性更高。

Narrative

The current US macro regime is Goldilocks: growth is picking up while core inflation is cooling, allowing the Fed to remain on hold for now. This has kept equities buoyant and credit spreads tight, with the S&P 500 at all-time highs. However, the labour market is showing signs of cooling, with monthly job gains falling to 57,000 in June, which could eventually cap growth.

The tension lies in the persistence of headline inflation above 4% (CPI 4.27%, PCE 4.07%), which is forcing the Fed futures to price in rate hikes by year-end. Meanwhile, bond market volatility has plummeted, suggesting the market is broadly comfortable with the trajectory, but hedging costs are rising (demand for downside protection up), indicating institutional caution. The dollar's strength is also pressuring EM and funding currencies, creating divergence globally.

Trigger risk · If headline inflation fails to slow and the Fed actually delivers a hike, then equity valuations, especially expensive tech, may face a sharp repricing and the yen could strengthen rapidly as carry trades unwind.

Structural Backdrop
Where we stand in the big cycle

The US economy is in a mid-cycle Goldilocks regime that has persisted for several months, supported by accelerating growth and decelerating core inflation. The Fed's balance sheet is stable (total assets $6,736bn, +0.5% over 13 weeks), and net liquidity is flat at $5,956bn, while reverse repo has nearly drained to $6bn and M2 is expanding at a robust 5.6% year-on-year. The rate futures curve implies the Fed will hike by 26 basis points by year-end, pushing the implied rate to 3.96%, a hawkish repricing from current levels. The yield curve is in a bear steepener with the 2-10 year spread at 62bp, reflecting expectations of higher growth and inflation premia at the long end. This structural backdrop suggests a transition from the peak liquidity phase towards a tightening cycle—investors should prepare for gradually rising real rates and a more selective environment where bonds may underperform and equity leadership could rotate away from duration-sensitive sectors over a multi-year horizon.

Historical Context

The current setup most closely echoes mid-2007, when the macro regime was similarly Goldilocks with low volatility and tight credit spreads. Back then, the Fed was on hold, equities were at highs, and complacency was widespread, but the yield curve was flattening and hedging demand was rising quietly. Today's key similarity is the combination of strong risk appetite, low vol, and tight credit, with institutional hedging activity (rising SKEW) hinting at underpriced tail risk. The most important difference is that the curve is steepening, not flat, and the banking system is much better capitalized. If this episode follows the 2007 path, we should watch for a sudden repricing of credit risk that could hit equities hard, but the current macro momentum suggests a soft landing remains more likely.
