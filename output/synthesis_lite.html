完整研判分析

美國經濟處於甜蜜點：成長加速，核心通膨溫和回落，為風險資產創造理想環境。這推升標普500至歷史新高，並將信用利差壓縮至罕見低位。然而，債券市場開始定價不同的現實——期貨暗示聯準會將在年底前升息。結果是美元飆升，現已成為全球市場的主導力量。

緊張點在於脫節：美國股市興高采烈，但強勢美元正從世界其他地區吸走資金。台灣面臨歷史性流出，日圓利差交易承受嚴重壓力，即使在表面波動率下降之際，對沖成本仍在上升。這種分歧不會永遠持續。若美元繼續走強，可能從最擁擠的部位開始，觸發廣泛的避險事件。

觸發風險 · 若美元續漲且聯準會明確暗示升息，預期新興市場及日圓融資的利差交易將急遽反轉。

長期結構定位
現在在大週期的哪裡

聯準會資產負債表規模為6.7兆美元且小幅擴張，但過去三個月淨流動性大致持平——逆回購餘額近乎歸零，銀行準備金微幅下滑。利率期貨曲線則講述了更決定性的故事：市場已定價2026年12月完全升息，儘管聯準會本身仍按兵不動。殖利率曲線正以熊平方式趨平，兩年與十年期利差約59基點，短期利率上升速度快於長期。結構上，我們處於升息循環的後期階段，市場正推動聯準會行動，儘管官方尚未行動。對跨數年投資期的投資人而言，此背景有利於能承受強勢美元與較高前端利率的資產——美國股票與實質資產——而長天期債券與利率敏感類股則面臨逆風。

歷史借鏡解讀

當前時刻最接近2007年中——一段低波動、信用利差緊縮、股市自滿的時期。當時聯準會同樣按兵不動，強勢美元開始對全球資金流動構成壓力。關鍵相似處在於低波動與極端部位的組合。最大的不同是當今的經濟基本面更強，成長加速、核心通膨下滑，這可能延長金髮女孩時期。若這次循類似路徑，我們可能看到由全球資金緊縮引發的突然波動飆升——但若成長持穩，痛苦可能僅限於最擁擠的交易。

Narrative

The US economy is in a sweet spot: growth is accelerating, and core inflation is gently retreating, creating ideal conditions for risk assets. This has pushed the S&P 500 to record highs and compressed credit spreads to levels rarely seen. However, the bond market is beginning to price a different reality, with futures implying the Fed will raise rates by year-end. The result is a surging dollar, which is now the dominant force in global markets.

The tension lies in the disconnect: US stocks are euphoric, but the strong dollar is siphoning capital out of the rest of the world. Taiwan is seeing historic outflows, the yen carry trade is under severe strain, and hedging costs are rising even as headline volatility falls. This divergence cannot last forever. If the dollar continues to rally, it could trigger a broad-based risk-off event, starting with the most crowded positions.

Trigger risk · If the dollar keeps climbing and the Fed explicitly signals a hike, expect sharp reversals in emerging markets and yen-funded carry trades.

Structural Backdrop
Where we stand in the big cycle

The Federal Reserve’s balance sheet stands at $6.7 trillion and is expanding slightly, but net liquidity has been essentially flat over the past three months—reverse repo balances have nearly vanished, and bank reserves have dipped slightly. The rate futures curve tells a more decisive story: the market is pricing a full rate hike by December 2026, even though the Fed itself remains on hold. The yield curve is flattening in a bearish manner, with the two-to-ten year spread around 59 basis points and short-term rates rising faster than long-term rates. Structurally, we are in a late-cycle tightening environment, where the market is pushing the Fed to act despite official inaction. For investors over a multi-year horizon, this backdrop favors assets that can withstand a strong dollar and higher front-end rates—US equities and real assets—while long-duration bonds and rate-sensitive sectors face headwinds.

Historical Context

The current moment most closely echoes mid-2007—a period of low volatility, tight credit spreads, and complacency in equity markets. Back then, the Fed was also on hold, and a strong dollar began to strain global capital flows. The key similarity is the combination of low vol and extreme positioning. The crucial difference is that today’s economic fundamentals are arguably stronger, with accelerating growth and falling core inflation, which could extend the Goldilocks period. If this episode follows a similar path, we might see a sudden volatility spike driven by a global funding squeeze—but if growth holds up, the pain may remain contained to the most crowded trades.
