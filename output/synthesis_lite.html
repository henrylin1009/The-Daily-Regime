歷史借鏡解讀

當前環境最像2007年中與2018年初。2007年，殖利率曲線平坦、波動低、信貸市場自滿，隨後爆發全球金融危機。2018年初，聯準會緊縮、曲線開始倒掛，導致當年稍後股市大幅修正。今日關鍵相似處在於曲線趨平、波動上升、信貸利差極度狹窄。最重要的差異是通膨遠高於當時（CPI 4.02%，2007與2018年約2%），限制了聯準會在成長放緩時寬鬆的空間。若依循2007年模式，信貸利差會先擴大，股市隨後下跌；但若通膨持續黏著，聯準會可能被迫繼續緊縮，使衰退更深。

Historical Context

The current setup most closely echoes mid-2007 and early 2018. In 2007, the yield curve was flat, volatility was low, and credit markets were complacent—just before the global financial crisis. In early 2018, the Fed was tightening and the curve was beginning to invert, leading to a sharp equity correction later that year. The key similarity today is the combination of a flattening curve, rising volatility, and extremely tight credit spreads. The most important difference is that inflation is much higher now (CPI at 4.02% vs. around 2% in 2007 and 2018), which limits the Fed's ability to ease if growth falters. If this episode follows the 2007 pattern, we would see credit spreads widen first, then equities roll over—but if inflation stays sticky, the Fed may be forced to keep tightening, making the downturn deeper.
