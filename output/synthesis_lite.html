完整研判分析

美國宏觀環境確實有利——增長加速，核心通脹降溫。聯準會按兵不動，信貸利差歷史低位，股市創新高。這正是金髮女孩的本質，風險資產如魚得水。

然而全球畫面沒那麼祥和。美元本週升逾1%，日圓跌至162，台幣創低。小型開放經濟體資金外逃，外匯期貨部位極端——日圓與英鎊大規模空頭，歐元與美債過度多頭——加劇了突然平倉的風險。若美元繼續走強，將進一步收緊全球金融環境，可能打擊新興市場並反噬美股。公債曲線熊平與市場計入12月加息，令此張力升溫。

觸發風險 · 若美元續升且殖利率曲線進一步扁平，則風險資產，特別是新興市場及套利交易，恐面臨修正。

長期結構定位
現在在大週期的哪裡

儘管聯準會資產負債表微幅擴張（13週增0.5%至6.74兆美元），淨流動性幾乎持平於5.96兆美元，因財政部一般帳戶與隔夜逆回購吸收增量流動。聯準會按兵不動於3.63%，但利率期貨已計入12月前升息至3.93%，反映市場對經濟持續強勁的期待。殖利率曲線呈熊平，2-10年利差僅+0.62百分點，屬週期中後段典型。此結構背景下，流動性緩步收緊而利率偏向上行，預示多年的寬鬆貨幣環境轉向。對投資者而言，此環境有利於受惠利率上升與通脹的策略，如短存續期資產及實質資產，長債與高槓桿板塊則面臨逆風。

Narrative

The US macro backdrop is genuinely favorable — growth is accelerating, and core inflation is cooling. The Fed is on hold, credit spreads are historically tight, and the stock market is at record highs. This is the essence of a Goldilocks environment, where risk assets thrive.

However, the global picture is less benign. The US dollar has surged over 1% this week, pushing the yen to 162 and the Taiwan dollar to new lows. Capital is fleeing small open economies, and extreme positioning in FX futures — massive shorts in yen and pound, crowded longs in euros and Treasuries — raises the risk of a sharp unwinding. If the dollar continues to strengthen, it will tighten global financial conditions further, potentially hurting emerging markets and feeding back into US equities. The bear flattening of the yield curve and the market pricing a rate hike by December add to this tension.

Trigger risk · If the dollar continues to rally and the yield curve flattens further, then risk assets, especially in emerging markets and carry trades, could face a correction.

Structural Backdrop
Where we stand in the big cycle

Despite the Fed's balance sheet expanding modestly (up 0.5% over the past 13 weeks to $6.74 trillion), net liquidity is essentially flat at $5.96 trillion, as the Treasury General Account and overnight reverse repo absorb incremental flows. The Fed is on hold at 3.63%, but rate futures price a hike by December to 3.93%, reflecting market expectations of sustained economic strength. The yield curve is in a bear flattener, with the 2-10 year spread at a modest +0.62 percentage points — typical of a mid-to-late cycle. This structural backdrop, with liquidity tightening slowly and rates biased higher, suggests a multi-year transition away from easy money. For investors, this favors strategies that benefit from rising rates and inflation, such as short-duration assets and real assets, while long-duration bonds and over-leveraged sectors face headwinds.

Historical Context

Today's backdrop echoes mid-2007, when a flat yield curve, low volatility, and credit complacency masked growing systemic risks. Back then, the US housing bubble was about to burst, but markets remained calm until the subprime losses surfaced. The key similarity is the combination of tight credit spreads and a flattening yield curve against a seemingly strong economy. The crucial difference is that today's banking system is more resilient, and inflation — not a housing bubble — is the central concern. If this episode follows a similar path, watch for a sudden spike in volatility and credit stress, though the current Goldilocks growth and cooling inflation may provide a buffer.
