完整研判分析

美國經濟正在過熱。通膨正在加速，消費者物價指數年增率達 4.08%，遠高於聯準會的舒適區。成長也在回升，勞動市場依然緊俏。這迫使聯準會放棄任何降息念頭，轉而暗示進一步升息。期貨市場目前預期到 2027 年中將再升息三次，隱含利率升至 4.71%。這是典型的過熱狀態：需求強勁、物價上漲、央行試圖降溫。

關鍵的緊張關係在於股市創新高與升息預期升溫之間的矛盾。標普 500 指數處於歷史區間的第 99 百分位，但 10 年期美債殖利率已達 5.10% 且仍在攀升。信用利差處於第 4 百分位——極度收窄——顯示投資人認為沒有違約風險。但殖利率曲線正在變陡，歷史上這意味著市場預期聯準會將持續升息，直到某些東西斷裂。美元飆升正在擠壓全球其他地區，日本和歐洲出現資本外流，台灣更出現創紀錄的外資賣超。美國強勢與全球壓力之間的分歧，是最需要關注的重點。

觸發風險 · 若 10 年期美債殖利率突破 5.25% 且美元持續飆升，則新興市場和高收益信用將出現急劇修正。

歷史借鏡解讀

目前的環境最像 2018 年末，當時聯準會正在緊縮，殖利率曲線開始倒掛。當時美元走強，新興市場遭到拋售，股市最終在第四季急劇修正。關鍵的相似之處在於：聯準會偏鷹、美元強勢、升息預期升溫，而股市處於歷史高點。最重要的差異是，今天的通膨遠高於當時——4.08% 對比 2018 年的約 2%——而且聯準會落後於曲線的程度更大。如果這個情節遵循類似的路徑，我們可能在未來 3-6 個月看到股市急劇修正和資金湧向避險資產，但風險在於通膨比預期更黏著，迫使聯準會更激進地升息，導致更深的衰退。

Narrative

The US economy is running hot. Inflation is accelerating, with the consumer price index at 4.08% year-over-year, well above the Fed's comfort zone. Growth is also picking up, and the labor market remains tight. This has forced the Fed to abandon any thought of rate cuts and instead signal more hikes. The futures market now prices in three additional rate increases by mid-2027, pushing the implied rate to 4.71%. This is a classic Overheat regime: strong demand, rising prices, and a central bank trying to cool things down.

The key tension is between record stock prices and rising rate expectations. The S&P 500 is at the 99th percentile of its historical range, yet the 10-year Treasury yield is at 5.10% and climbing. Credit spreads are at the 4th percentile—extremely tight—suggesting investors see no default risk. But the yield curve is steepening, which historically signals that the market expects the Fed to keep hiking until something breaks. The dollar's surge is squeezing the rest of the world, with Japan and Europe seeing capital outflows and Taiwan experiencing record foreign selling. This divergence between US strength and global stress is the most important thing to watch.

Trigger risk · If the 10-year Treasury yield breaks above 5.25% and the dollar continues to surge, then expect a sharp correction in emerging markets and high-yield credit.

Structural Backdrop
Where we stand in the big cycle

We are in the late stage of a global financial cycle, with the US as the anchor. The Fed's balance sheet is stable at $6,759 billion, but net liquidity is flat, and the central bank is signaling more rate hikes. The futures curve implies three additional hikes by mid-2027, pushing the policy rate to 4.71%. The yield curve is steepening, with the 2-10 year spread at +0.38 percentage points, which historically signals that the market expects the Fed to over-tighten and cause a slowdown. This structural backdrop—tightening financial conditions, a strong dollar, and rising real rates—is a headwind for long-duration assets and emerging markets over a multi-year horizon. Investors should prepare for a period of higher volatility and lower returns as the cycle matures.

Historical Context

The current setup most closely echoes late 2018, when the Fed was tightening and the yield curve was beginning to invert. Back then, the dollar strengthened, emerging markets sold off, and stocks eventually corrected sharply in the fourth quarter. The key similarity is the combination of a hawkish Fed, a strong dollar, and rising rate expectations against a backdrop of record stock prices. The most important difference is that today's inflation is much higher—4.08% versus around 2% in 2018—and the Fed is further behind the curve. If this episode follows a similar path, we could see a sharp equity correction and a flight to safety in the next 3-6 months, but the risk is that inflation proves stickier than expected, forcing the Fed to hike even more aggressively and causing a deeper downturn.
