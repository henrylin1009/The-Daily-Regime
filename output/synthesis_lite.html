美元強勢回歸

i美元指數一個月內升值超過2%，衡量動能的數值來到極端水準。日圓處於數十年低點，投機性淨空單達到極端。美元飆升當日，日本及台灣股市分別大跌4%與3.6%。此局面常引發套利交易重新定價及新興市場資金外流。

美元強勁反彈，日圓與台幣大幅貶值，全球市場餘波盪漾。市場預期美國將進一步升息，而其他主要央行按兵不動，推升美元走勢。此趨勢打擊亞洲股市，並可能衝擊新興市場企業的財務體質。

金髮女孩動搖

i利率期貨曲線顯示自目前3.63%至2026年底將升息0.30%。量化引擎將「聯準會路徑衝擊」列為風險。核心通膨降至2.96%，但整體CPI仍達4.27%，就業市場新增17.2萬人仍屬穩健。長期名目債與抗通膨債的利差惡化，反映債市通膨預期升溫。

儘管官方數據仍顯示經濟成長與通膨處於金髮女孩組合，市場卻愈發傾向認為聯準會必須升息。聯邦基金期貨現已隱含年底前升息0.30%。此重新定價與典型的「不過熱、不過冷」環境相悖。若成長保持強勁，核心通膨降得不夠快，此局面可能轉向過熱。

長線意義 · 此環境不利長天期債券與高成長股，而有利於週期股與價值股。

完整研判分析

美國經濟仍處於令人羨慕的地位：成長加速，核心通膨溫和回落。這種『金髮女孩』組合一直是風險資產的強大燃料，將企業借貸成本壓至十年低點，股市推至歷史新高。然而，市場開始質疑此榮景能否持續。短期利率期貨現正定價年底前至少再升息一次，顯示債市投資人認為過熱風險比衰退風險更為迫切。

今日最顯著的壓力跡象是美元聲勢浩大的反彈。強勢美元會增加外國借款人的美元債務成本，並壓縮美國跨國企業的獲利，從而抑制全球成長。它也會在最擁擠的交易中引發連鎖反應，尤其是日圓套利交易，此前投資人大舉放空日圓以融資進行其他投資。若美元繼續攀升，我們可能看到風險資產遭強制拋售和無序的去槓桿。目前市場情緒保持審慎樂觀，但裂縫正在擴大。

觸發風險 · 若美元繼續飆升，且美國以外的全球股市開始崩跌，那可能標誌著從金髮女孩轉向更不利的環境，潛在引發更廣泛的市場修正。

長期結構定位
現在在大週期的哪裡

聯準會資產負債表處於緩慢擴張階段，但因銀行準備金小幅減少，整體流動性環境在過去一季大致持平。殖利率曲線呈『牛市陡峭化』形態，意味短期利率持穩而長天期收益率因成長預期微幅走高。利率期貨定價從暫停轉向溫和緊縮週期，預期年底前升息0.30%。此種景氣循環末期的背景顯示，雖然美國資產可能仍將受惠於相對經濟強勢，犯錯空間卻正在縮小。投資人應為成長或通膨意外引發的格局轉變做好準備。

Narrative

The US economy remains in an enviable position: growth is picking up speed while core inflation is gently receding. This 'Goldilocks' mix has been a powerful fuel for risk assets, pushing corporate borrowing costs to decade lows and equities to record highs. However, the market is beginning to question whether this can last. Short-term interest rate futures are now pricing at least one additional Federal Reserve rate increase by year-end, a sign that the bond crowd sees the risk of overheating as more pressing than the risk of recession.

The standout sign of strain today is the US dollar’s thunderous rally. A stronger dollar acts as a brake on global growth by making dollar-denominated debt more expensive for foreign borrowers and by compressing US multinational earnings. It also sets off chain reactions in the most crowded trades, particularly the yen carry trade, where investors have been heavily short the Japanese yen to fund bets elsewhere. If the dollar continues to climb, we could see forced selling in risk assets and a disorderly unwind. For now, the mood is one of cautious optimism, but the cracks are growing.

Trigger risk · If the dollar continues its surge and global equities outside the US start to break down, then it could signal a shift from Goldilocks to a more hostile environment, potentially triggering a broader market correction.

Structural Backdrop
Where we stand in the big cycle

The Federal Reserve’s balance sheet is in a slow expansion phase, but the overall liquidity environment is roughly flat over the past quarter due to a modest decline in bank reserves. The yield curve is in a 'bull steepener' formation, meaning short-term rates are anchored while long-term yields inch higher on growth expectations. Rate futures are pricing a transition from pause to a mild tightening cycle, with 0.30% of rate hikes expected by year-end. This late-cycle backdrop suggests that while US assets may still benefit from relative economic strength, the margin for error is narrowing. Investors should be prepared for a regime shift if growth or inflation surprises.

Historical Context

The current setup most closely echoes May 2007, a period marked by a flat to steepening yield curve, extremely low volatility, and credit spreads so tight they signaled investor complacency. Back then, a strong dollar was not the theme, but the macro calm masked building imbalances that erupted later. Today’s key similarity is the extreme tightness in credit spreads and the low volatility despite underlying tensions. The crucial difference is that the Fed is now pricing in hikes, not cuts, and the global economy is less leveraged. If this episode follows a similar path, we could see a sharp repricing of risk assets within 12 months—but the immediate trigger remains elusive, and a strong US economy might delay the reckoning.
