美國

債市波動消失，但利率收緊

iMOVE 指數暴跌 10.94% 至 65.40，為數月最低，market volatility 也同步回落。與此同時，美國 10 年期收益率週線上漲 2.28% 至 4.48%，聯邦基金期貨定價年底前升息 26 個基點。殖利率曲線熊平（2y10y 利差 0.36%），屬典型週期末段的收緊傾向。當前為金髮女孩狀態：增長加速、通膨降溫（CPI 從 2.99% 降至 2.96%），但債市已開始為升息定價。

債市波動率跌至接近紀錄低位，顯示投資者完全相信金髮女孩敘事——增長加速、通膨降溫、聯準會按兵不動。但 10 年期收益率上升，期貨市場定價年底前升息一次，這種微妙的收緊最終可能壓制風險資產。

長線意義 · 低波動率與利率上升的緊張組合，有利於短天期債券和優質股票，不利於長天期債券和投機級信貸。

長線意義 · 日圓突然回升將打擊槓桿利差交易，並可能引發廣泛的避險事件，壓制新興市場和高貝塔資產。

完整研判分析

美國經濟仍處於金髮女孩甜蜜點：增長加速（GDP 2.68%），通膨終於降溫（CPI 從 2.99% 降至 2.96%）。聯準會按兵不動在 3.63%，債市僅定價年底前升息一次。這使得股市波動率偏低，信用利差收窄至歷史極窄。market volatility 處於第 46 百分位——冷靜但非自滿。這是風險資產緩步走高的典型配方。

然而，表面之下張力正在累積。美元在風險偏好上升之際反而走強，受巨大利差驅動——較日本高 193 基點、較歐洲高 153 基點。貨幣持倉極端：日圓淨空頭為多年之最，英鎊淨空頭接近紀錄。若美元漲勢逆轉或日圓突然回升，槓桿利差交易可能猛烈平倉，引發劇烈避險事件。債市波動率跌至近零也是警告——它往往先於波動率飆升。

觸發風險 · 若日圓一週內升值超過 2%（例如因日本央行干預或避險情緒），槓桿利差交易將平倉，導致美股急跌、market volatility 飆升。

長期結構定位
現在在大週期的哪裡

聯準會資產負債表規模 6,725 億美元，過去一季大致持平（扣除財政部帳戶和逆回購後，淨流動性增加 140 億美元）。逆回購幾近耗盡（45 億美元），代表原先停放在聯準會的現金大多已回流市場——這是一項支撐性流動性條件。然而，銀行準備金 13 週來下降 4.8%，構成微妙的緊縮。利率期貨曲線定價 2026 年底前升息一次，2027 年 3 月再升一次，反映在金髮女孩敘事下的鷹派傾向。殖利率曲線熊平（2 年對 10 年利差 0.36%），為典型的週期末段訊號。整體而言，此結構背景顯示聯準會維持限制性立場，歷史經驗最終會導致放緩——但就目前而言，流動性仍足夠支撐風險資產。

歷史借鏡解讀

當前設定最接近 2007 年中（距離 1.75），當時殖利率曲線平坦、波動率低、信用利差窄——看似平靜的金融危機前夕。關鍵相似之處在於 VIX 偏低、債市波動率（MOVE）接近紀錄低位，以及日本極端的利差交易活動。但關鍵差異在於美國增長加速（GDP 2.68%）、通膨降溫，而 2007 年正值房市主導的放緩。若遵循 2007 年路徑，留意信用事件或利差交易突然平倉作為觸發點。但更健康的增長背景降低了重演的可能性；反而 2024 年類比（距離 3.9）顯示金髮女孩格局可能再持續數月，主要風險是聯準會緊縮過度。

Narrative

The US economy remains in a Goldilocks sweet spot: growth is accelerating (2.68% GDP) while inflation is finally decelerating (CPI dropping from 2.99% to 2.96%). The Fed is on hold at 3.63%, and the bond market is pricing in just one hike by year-end. This has kept equity volatility low and pushed credit spreads to historic tights. The market volatility is at the 46th percentile—calm but not complacent. This is a classic recipe for risk assets to grind higher.

However, beneath the surface, tensions are building. The dollar is strengthening even as risk appetite rises, driven by massive yield gaps—193bp over Japan, 153bp over Europe. Currency positioning is extreme: the yen is the most short it has been in years, and pound shorts are near records. If the dollar rally reverses or the yen suddenly strengthens, leveraged carry trades could unwind violently, triggering a sharp risk-off event. The collapse in bond volatility to near-zero levels is also a warning—it often precedes a volatility spike.

Trigger risk · If the yen strengthens by more than 2% in a week (e.g., due to BoJ intervention or risk-off), leveraged carry trades will unwind, causing a sharp drop in US equities and a spike in the market volatility.

Structural Backdrop
Where we stand in the big cycle

The Federal Reserve's balance sheet stands at $6,725 billion, roughly flat over the past quarter (+14 billion in net liquidity after adjusting for the Treasury General Account and reverse repo). Reverse repo is virtually empty at $4.5 billion, meaning cash that was parked at the Fed has largely returned to the market—a supportive liquidity condition. However, bank reserves have declined 4.8% over 13 weeks, a subtle tightening. The rate futures curve prices a hike by year-end 2026 and another by March 2027, reflecting a hawkish tilt despite the Goldilocks narrative. The yield curve is bear-flattening (2-year vs 10-year spread at 0.36%), a classic late-cycle signal. Overall, this structural backdrop suggests the Fed is staying restrictive, which historically leads to a slowdown eventually—but for now, liquidity is still ample enough to support risk assets.

Historical Context

The current setup most closely echoes mid-2007 (distance 1.75), when the yield curve was flat, volatility was low, and credit spreads were tight—a period of seeming calm before the financial crisis. The key similarity is the combination of low VIX, near-record-low bond volatility (MOVE), and extreme carry trade activity in Japan. However, the critical difference is that US growth is accelerating (2.68% GDP) and inflation is decelerating, whereas 2007 saw a housing-led slowdown. If this follows the 2007 pattern, watch for a credit event or a sudden unwind of carry trades as the trigger. But the healthier growth backdrop makes a direct repeat less likely; instead, the 2024 analogue (distance 3.9) suggests the Goldilocks regime can persist for months longer, with the main risk being a Fed that tightens too much.
