歷史借鏡解讀

當前環境最接近2007年5月，當時殖利率曲線平坦、波動率低、信貸市場自滿。當時聯準會在強勁經濟中收緊政策，次貸信貸首先出現裂縫，隨後蔓延至更廣泛的市場。今日的關鍵相似點是極端股市估值、信貸惡化與殖利率曲線平坦化並存。最重要的差異在於今日的壓力在高收益債與債市波動，而非房地產，且聯準會有更多應對空間。若此情節循類似路徑發展，應關注信貸利差進一步擴大與美元持續飆升，這將壓抑風險資產——但聯準會迅速轉鴿可能改寫劇本。

Historical Context

The current setup most closely echoes May 2007, when the yield curve was flat, volatility was low, and credit markets were complacent. Back then, the Fed was tightening into a strong economy, and the first cracks appeared in subprime credit before spreading to the broader market. The key similarity today is the combination of extreme equity valuations, deteriorating credit, and a flattening yield curve. The most important difference is that today's stress is in high-yield credit and bond volatility, not housing, and the Fed has more room to respond. If this episode follows a similar path, we should watch for credit spreads to widen further and the dollar to keep surging, which would pressure risk assets — but a rapid Fed pivot could change the script.
