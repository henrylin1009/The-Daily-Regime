歷史借鏡解讀

當前環境最接近 2007 年 5 月，當時殖利率曲線平坦、波動率低、信用市場自滿，隨後爆發全球金融危機。當時聯準會按兵不動，信用利差緊縮，股市平靜，但房市與信用市場的壓力正在累積。今日的關鍵相似處在於股市創紀錄高點、信用利差緊縮、殖利率曲線平坦化，且債市波動上升顯示隱藏風險。最重要的差異在於今日的壓力來自全球資本流動與極端部位，而非房市泡沫。若此情節循類似路徑，未來 3-12 個月風險資產可能出現劇烈重定價，但關鍵風險是聯準會的鷹派轉變可能觸發比 2007 年更快的平倉。

Historical Context

The current setup most closely echoes May 2007, a period of flat yield curve, low volatility, and credit complacency before the global financial crisis. Back then, the Fed was on hold, credit spreads were tight, and equity markets were calm, but underlying stress was building in the housing and credit markets. The key similarity today is the combination of record stock prices, tight credit spreads, and a flattening yield curve, with rising bond volatility signaling hidden risk. The most important difference is that today's stress is emerging from global capital flows and extreme positioning rather than a housing bubble. If this episode follows a similar path, we could see a sharp repricing in risk assets over the next 3-12 months, but a key risk is that the Fed's hawkish shift could trigger a faster unwind than in 2007.
