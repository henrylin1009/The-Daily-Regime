完整研判分析

美國經濟處於過熱體制——成長加速、通膨上升，CPI達3.95%，PCE達3.77%。但股市上漲、波動率偏低、信用利差緊縮。這看似金髮女孩情境：經濟強勁足以支撐獲利，但又不至於迫使聯準會大幅緊縮。殖利率曲線陡化，歷史上這發生在市場預期成長持續之時。

張力在於通膨加速的速度比市場定價更快。分歧分數偏高，對沖成本異常顯示機構正在悄悄買入保護，即使表面波動平靜。日圓空頭處於極端，過去曾預示劇烈反轉。同時，台灣出現突然的外資流出，可能預示亞洲科技股的麻煩。若通膨迫使聯準會升息，或日圓套利交易平倉，當前的平靜可能迅速打破。

觸發風險 · 若美國通膨持續加速（CPI超過4%）或日圓一週內突然升值超過3%，則風險資產可能因套利交易平倉和聯準會被迫行動而大幅下跌。

Today's Themes
The themes and risks that matter most today
US

Goldilocks holds, but cracks are forming

iThe US macro regime is Overheat: growth accelerating, CPI at 3.95% (89th percentile) and rising, PCE at 3.77% (93rd percentile). Yet the S&P 500 is near all-time highs and market volatility is low (15.4, 36th percentile). The yield curve is steepening (2y10y at 42bp), credit spreads are tight (Baa at 1.54%, 8th percentile). This combination of rising inflation and low volatility is historically fragile — the closest analogue is 2007-11 (pre-GFC), which saw a sharp equity decline 12 months later.

The US economy is growing and inflation is accelerating, yet the stock market is rallying and volatility is low. This looks like a Goldilocks scenario, but inflation at 3.95% and rising is a warning sign that the Fed may need to act.

Long-term takeaway · This environment historically favours equities and real assets over long-duration bonds.

Japan · US

Yen positioning at a dangerous extreme

iJPY net shorts are at -58,747 contracts, with a 52-week z-score of -2.56 — an extreme rarely seen. The yen has weakened 2.19% against the dollar in the past month, and the US-Japan rate spread is 196bp, encouraging carry trades. The the Australian dollar carry trade cross is also elevated. Historical analogues (e.g., 2007-09) show that when yen shorts are this extreme, a sudden reversal can cause sharp losses in carry trades and spill over into equities.

Almost everyone is betting against the yen — net shorts are at a level seen only a few times in recent history. The yen has already weakened 2.2% against the dollar this month, and the carry trade is crowded.

Long-term takeaway · A sudden yen rally could trigger a violent unwind, pressuring risk assets globally.

Taiwan

Foreign investors pulled a large amount of money out of Taiwan in the last five days, the biggest outflow in months. The Taiwan dollar is also weakening, suggesting the selloff is broad-based.

Narrative

The US economy is in an Overheat regime — growth is accelerating and inflation is rising, with CPI at 3.95% and PCE at 3.77%. Yet the stock market is rallying, volatility is low, and credit spreads are tight. This looks like a Goldilocks scenario where the economy is strong enough to support earnings but not so hot that the Fed needs to tighten aggressively. The yield curve is steepening, which historically happens when the market expects growth to continue.

The tension is that inflation is accelerating faster than the market seems to price. The the gap between markets is elevated, and the hedging cost anomaly suggests institutions are quietly buying protection even as surface volatility looks calm. The yen short is at an extreme that has preceded sharp reversals in the past. Meanwhile, Taiwan is seeing a sudden foreign outflow that could signal trouble for Asian tech. If inflation forces the Fed to hike, or if the yen carry trade unwinds, the current calm could break quickly.

Trigger risk · If US inflation continues to accelerate (CPI above 4%) or the yen suddenly strengthens by more than 3% in a week, then risk assets could sell off sharply as the carry trade unwinds and the Fed is forced to act.
