完整研判分析

我們仍處於金髮女郎狀態：經濟成長加速，核心通膨降溫。歷史上看，這是風險資產的甜蜜點，事實上股市處於歷史新高，信用利差接近歷史低點，市場恐慌指數平靜。表面上看，一切像是完美的軟著陸。

但在這平靜的表面下，緊張正在累積。整體通膨仍高，期貨市場定價年底前升息，儘管聯準會表示按兵不動。這推升美元並壓低了債市波動，暗示鷹派重新定價。日圓利差交易處於極端，投機者大舉放空日圓，提高突然平倉的風險。同時，大錢正悄悄買進下檔保護，即使股市創高，顯示日益謹慎。若聯準會驗證市場的升息擔憂，可能擾亂金髮女郎敘事。

觸發風險 · 若聯準會因頑固的整體通膨而暗示升息，長天期債券及日圓融資利差交易可能遭受急遽逆轉。

長期結構定位
現在在大週期的哪裡

聯準會的資產負債表穩定，淨流動性大致持平，因逆回購機制已枯竭而準備金仍然充裕。然而，M2 貨幣供給年增逾 5%，提供支撐背景。利率期貨曲線定價年底前幾乎確定升息，逆轉「按兵不動」立場。殖利率曲線呈熊市平坦化，兩年與十年期公債利差收窄，因短期利率隨緊縮預期攀升。此結構背景暗示，雖然流動性仍具支撐，但趨勢正轉向約束；多年來看，除非成長令人失望，投資人應預期金融狀況趨緊。

歷史借鏡解讀

當前格局最接近 2019 年末，當時聯準會維持利率不變、美元走軟，但主要經濟體貨幣政策分歧正在收窄。然而，今日情況也帶有 2007 年中期的色彩，殖利率曲線平坦、波動低、信貸自滿。2007 年時，這些情況領先了劇烈的緊縮衝擊。與 2007 年最相似的是利差交易部位的極端與波動的自滿。不同之處在於通膨正在降溫，且聯準會並未積極升息。若這輪走勢遵循類似 2007 年的路徑，美元或利率突然飆升可能引發顯著的股市拉回——但鑑於強韌的成長背景，這並非基本情境。

Today's Pulse
The most important market move today
Bond vol plunges, dollar surges as rate fears resurface

The cost of hedging U.S. government bond swings fell 10.9% today, while the dollar leapt 1.7% against a basket of currencies. Markets are pricing in a greater chance of a Fed rate hike by year-end, threatening the Goldilocks calm.

Stocks rallied 3% this week to an all-time high, with the S&P 500 at a level seen in the top percentile of its history. Meanwhile, the fear gauge dropped 14% to its calmest in over a year. High-yield credit spreads sit at historically tight levels, near the 8th percentile. However, beneath the surface, bond market volatility plunged on the day by nearly 11% and the U.S. dollar surged, suggesting that interest rate expectations are hardening. Investors are increasingly betting that the Federal Reserve may need to raise rates later this year, despite an official hold. This creates a tension: equities and credit are priced for a perfect soft landing, but the bond and currency markets are flashing tightening risks.

Narrative

We remain in a Goldilocks regime: economic growth is accelerating, and core inflation is cooling. This has historically been a sweet spot for risk assets, and indeed stocks are at record highs, credit spreads are near historic lows, and the market's fear gauge is calm. On the surface, everything looks ideal for a soft landing.

But beneath this placid surface, tensions are building. Headline inflation remains hot, and futures markets are pricing in a rate hike by year-end even though the Fed says it's on hold. This has shot the dollar higher and crushed bond market volatility, suggesting a hawkish repricing. The yen carry trade is at an extreme, with speculators heavily short the yen, raising the risk of a sudden unwind. Meanwhile, big money is quietly buying downside protection even as stocks hit highs, indicating growing caution. If the Fed validates the market's rate fears, it could disrupt the Goldilocks narrative.

Trigger risk · If the Federal Reserve signals a rate hike due to persistent headline inflation, then long-duration bonds and yen-funded carry trades could suffer sharp reversals.

Structural Backdrop
Where we stand in the big cycle

The Federal Reserve's balance sheet is stable, with net liquidity roughly flat as the reverse repo facility has drained and reserves remain ample. However, M2 money supply is expanding at over 5% a year, providing a supportive backdrop. The interest rate futures curve prices in a near-certain rate hike by year-end, reversing the 'on hold' stance. The yield curve is bear flattening, with the gap between two- and ten-year yields narrowing as short-term rates climb on expectations of tightening. This structural backdrop suggests that while liquidity is still supportive, the trend is shifting toward restraint; over a multi-year horizon, investors should expect tightening financial conditions unless growth disappoints.

Historical Context

The current setup most closely echoes late 2019, when the Fed held rates steady and the dollar softened, but monetary divergence between major economies was narrowing. However, today's situation also has shades of mid-2007, with a flat yield curve, low volatility, and credit complacency. In 2007, those conditions preceded a sharp tightening shock. The key similarity to 2007 is the extreme positioning in carry trades and the complacent volatility. The difference is that inflation is cooling, and the Fed is not actively hiking. If this episode follows a 2007-like path, a sudden spike in the dollar or rates could trigger a significant equity drawdown—but that is not a base case given the resilient growth backdrop.
